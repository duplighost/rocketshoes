Qualiacology · Toys

Little Toys

Small games, visual instruments, and strange little rooms to wander into for a minute or disappear inside for longer. Built to feel good on a phone and not look like a corpse on desktop.

Glide

Drift through an endless, sun-dappled forest as a tiny flying squirrel. Run up trees, leap, and glide on the wind. WASD + mouse, or two thumbs on a phone.

Play Glide

Quiet Hours

An endless golden-hour city to wander through. Open doors, climb through windows, crouch into rooms, and poke around without the game barking chores at you.

Wander the City

Spiggot Moonfeel

A tiny moon-body toy with cleaned-up directional movement, easier look control, and less of that cursed tank-control wobble.

Play Spiggot

The Screaming Banana Dilemma

A first-person surreal hallway problem with corrected look direction, smoother controls, and walls that no longer look like placeholder drywall from hell.

Face the Banana

LUMA

A living field of light that moves on its own and comes alive to your music or the mic. Touch paints it. It never looks the same twice.

Open LUMA

The Dispensary

The Dept. of Impossible Pharmacology is open. Press the capsule, receive a prescription for a compound that doesn't exist — full nomenclature, boxed warnings, infinite refills.

Fill a Prescription
